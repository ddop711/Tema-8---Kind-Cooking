Kind Cooking

About us

Here at KC (Kind Cooking) our goal is to help you gain confidence in the kitchen. We all know that learning a new skill can be scary and the fear of messing up can be a real paralyser, and cooking is no different. However, messing up doesn’t mean that you’ve failed but is an important part of learning. Besides wanting to make the cooking experience less daunting for everyone, we are also very passionate about lessening food waste and having affordable groceries for everyone. For that reason we have decided to partner up with the danish grocery store, “Wefood”, which you can read more about below.


About Wefood

“Shopping at Wefood has to be affordable. That has always been the goal. That is why we do our best to sell our wares cheaper than other grocery stores. Everyone who works at Wefood are volunteers, and all our profit goes to Folkekirkens Nødhjælps work. For that reason it is important for us, that Wefood is a responsible profit-business, so we can send money to people in vulnerable situations. you might experience, that our prices vary. The reason for this is that there are big differences between our wares. some things have cosmetic damages on the package, others are nearing their expiration-dates, and others are in the exact same condition they would be in, if you bought them at any other grocery store.” (This text was taken directly from Wefood’s own website and translated from danish to english by - Alex H. Subscribe to attend cooking livestreams

For $5 a month you can attend bi-monthly cooking livestreams, hosted by our talented colleges. For these livestreams, you will be provided with the all the necessary recipe information (what the dish is, all the needed ingredients, and all the needed appliances and cooking utensils) 4 days before the livestream. The people hosting the cooking livestream will focus on explaining all the steps in the recipe and taking everything at a slow pace.


Reasons these cooking livestreams could be useful: Since it is live, you will be able to ask questions about the recipe and receive live feedback from the hosts (or possibly from your fellow chatters). Sometimes it can be difficult to follow a recipe when you only have a picture and written instruction to go from. Watching someone make the dish while you follow along with the recipe can be a great help. You can share tips, questions, and experiences with all the other people who are present in the chat of the livestream.
